HelloID Beta

Which ID will you use?

Use your original, valid ID. Photocopies and photos of a screen are not accepted.

Issued in
    Your ID is already verified with HelloID.
    Your last HelloID check is waiting for GFTV's admins. Starting again replaces it.

    Before you start

    • You take a photo of your ID, and a short video check of your face: turn your head when asked, while the screen flashes colours. Find good light and hold your phone at arm's length.
    • GFTV's server checks the ID, that your face is live and matches the ID, and your device and connection. Nothing is sent to anyone else.
    • The photos are deleted as soon as the check is decided. If it passes, GFTV keeps a face template (numbers, not a picture) and a code made from your document's number, only so the same person cannot verify another account. They are deleted with your account.
    • If the checks are not sure, GFTV's admins look at the photos to decide.
    Turn your screen brightness all the way up before you start. The face check flashes colours on your screen and watches them light up your face: a brighter screen makes it much more likely to pass.

    HelloID could not read the lines of letters and <<< at the bottom of your ID clearly. Take the photo again:

    • Lay the ID flat on a plain, dark surface.
    • Use bright, even light, and tilt the ID or phone until there is no glare or shine on it.
    • Hold the phone straight above it, close enough to fill the frame, and wait for it to focus.

    Check your details

    Once your ID is verified, these become your locked legal name and date of birth.

    Now your face

    Fit your face in the oval and follow the prompts. Keep the phone still: move your head, not the phone.

    Take off hats, caps and other headwear, and your glasses unless your ID photo shows you wearing them. Never wear sunglasses. A religious head covering can stay on if your ID photo shows it, with your whole face in view.

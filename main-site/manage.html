    Basics
    events.globalfurry.tv/e/
    Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. Left blank, it is made from the title. Changing it breaks links already shared.

    People must give their country of origin on their account to RSVP or open the link, and the attendee list shows where everyone is from. Ticked for a convention to start with.

    Description

    The pasted text has lines in it:
    Description (English) Description (Chinese) Write it in both English and Chinese; switch between them above. Format it as in Word. Pasted text keeps its formatting, and pasted pictures are copied into the gallery. Click a picture in the description to size or crop it.
    Cover picture

    The cover is cut from a gallery picture: choose "Use as cover" on one below, then pick the 16:9 part to show. Event cards show it as their thumbnail.

    Gallery

    Add as many pictures as you like, up to 30. Each keeps its own shape, uncropped; your browser shrinks it into a small WebP before it is uploaded. The event page shows them under the description.

    Or copy a picture and paste it here with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).

    Pictures can be added once the event has saved.

      When

      Payment

      Set Price to Paid to choose how people pay.

      Tick every way you take. With a payment link or QR code, people get a ticket on a checkout page: it holds their place, and they tell you when they have paid. You then tick Paid on the attendee list.

      QR code

      A QR code can be added once the event has saved.

      Any shape works, uncropped. It is kept as sharp as it came, up to 1200 pixels on its longest side.

        Coming without a ticket

        For a paid event: set Price to Paid to use this.

        They are going like anyone else (address, RSVP ID, reminders) but take none of the event's spaces. They can tick meals too.

        RSVP and publishing

        They can tick "I'm a fursuiter" when they RSVP, and change it until the event. The attendee list shows who did, with a count.

        Contact details

        Ask the people going how to reach them. Tick any you want, or add your own. Only this event's editors see what they give, on the attendee list.

        Volunteers

        Only conventions take volunteers. Set Kind to Convention under Basics to use this.

        Only the people an editor ticked as "Volunteered" in that convention's attendee list get it. A percentage is up to 100; an amount is in this event's currency. Applying it at checkout comes later: for now the event page tells them they have it.

        There are no other conventions yet. Once there is one, it can be picked here.

        Registration form

        Questions people answer when they get a ticket, such as a badge name and T-shirt size. Each question can be asked of every tier, or only of some. People can change their answers until RSVPs close or their tier closes. Only this event's editors see them, on the attendee list.

        Coming just for a meal

        Tick the meals people can RSVP for on their own, instead of the whole event. Leave them all off to take RSVPs for the whole event only.

        Name your own, such as "High tea" or "Supper", up to five. People coming only for a meal do not take one of the event's spaces; give a meal a number of places to limit it, or leave Places blank for no limit.

        Timetable

        What happens when, for the event page. It shows there once it has an item, except while the dates are to be announced. People going are told when it changes.

        Rooms

        The timetable's columns, left to right: a hall, a panel room, a stage. A short tag such as "Level G" shows over the name. A meet in one place needs none.

        No rooms yet.

        What happens

        Nothing on the timetable yet.

        Times are in the event's time zone. An end earlier than the start is the next day (21:00 to 01:00). With no end, an item is shown as half an hour long. The event page puts the items in time order.

        Each step is a time and what happens then, such as "12:00: Travel to the border". Times are in the event's time zone. A step lasts until the next one; the event page puts them in time order.

        Staff notes

          Editors of this event

          People you add here can edit this event and manage its attendees, but cannot publish, cancel or delete it. They need two-step sign-in (an authenticator app or a passkey) turned on before it works.

            View page

            Venue sign-ups

            New accounts are limited to 5 an hour from one connection. At a meet or con where everyone shares the venue's Wi-Fi, open this page on that Wi-Fi and raise the limit for that connection for a few hours. It ends by itself.

              Name filter

              Words public accounts may not use in a username or display name. Matching ignores case, spacing, accents, look-alike letters and numbers used as letters.

              Always blocked:

                Existing names that match

                Names chosen before a word was listed are kept, and shown here.

                  Reset a member's password

                  For a member with no linked Telegram and no 2FA. Check it is really them first. The code is shown once, works once, and lasts 24 hours; they type it at events.globalfurry.tv/reset.

                  Correct a personal detail

                  A legal name, date of birth or country of origin is locked once someone sets it. Check it is really them first, then correct it here, or leave the new value blank to clear it so they can fill it in again. It is in the audit log.

                  Crop the cover picture

                  Drag the picture, or use the arrow keys, to move it. Zoom with the slider, the mouse wheel, a pinch, or the + and - keys. 0 goes back to the middle. The whole frame shows on event cards; the shaded strips at the top and bottom are left out of the wider banner on the event page, so keep faces and text between the dashed lines.
